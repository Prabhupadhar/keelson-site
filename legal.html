Privacy policy

This website and the Keelson Pro app use no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. Your project plan is read and turned into reports inside your browser tab; it is never uploaded to us or anyone else.

What the app keeps in your browser (localStorage on this device only, never sent to us): your theme choice; your brand colour, logo, client and "prepared by" names; your Pro licence key, its activation ID for this browser and when it was last checked; your status rules and re-baseline notes per project; your status call and its reason, milestone keywords, the milestones you left out and your working week and holidays (keelson-projects), per project; the last working week and holidays you used, as the default for new projects (keelson-calendar); and, when a version is saved, a snapshot of that report's figures (project name, task and phase names, finish dates and % complete) so the next report can show what changed (the 12 most recent per project). The runbook tool remembers your go/no-go ticks. Commentary you are still editing is kept in sessionStorage for this tab only and is gone when the tab closes.

Version history (IndexedDB on this device only, never sent to us): when you download a Word or PowerPoint report or press Save version, and each time you add an older file, the app saves a version of the plan for that project and status date, so it can show what changed, the slip trend and finish-date confidence. A version holds the project name; each task's name, ID, phase and owner (often people's names); its start, finish, baseline and actual dates, % complete and done/milestone flags; cancelled tasks; the overall status (Red/Amber/Green) and summary figures; and the file and sheet name it came from and when it was saved. A .keelson.json export contains all of this, so anyone you give the file to can read it. Up to 52 versions are kept per project; older ones are dropped. You can export a project's history as a .keelson.json file and import it on another computer; that file stays wherever you save it. Sample projects are kept in memory only, unless you download a report from one. Anyone using this browser profile can see this data. To remove it, use "Clear saved data" in the app (it also deletes every project's version history), or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Hosting. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser type when you load a page, and may keep them in its server logs under GitHub's privacy statement. Your plan is never part of those requests.

Licence checks. When you enter a Pro key, your browser sends it to our reseller Lemon Squeezy (USA, api.lemonsqueezy.com) with a label naming this browser (for example "Keelson · Chrome · 2026-10-24") to activate it here; Lemon Squeezy returns an activation ID, which counts this browser towards the key's limit. About once a week after that, the key and that activation ID are sent to confirm the key is still valid, and removing the key from a browser sends them once more to free the slot. As with any web request, your IP address and browser type are seen too. No plan data is included. Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy applies to that check.

The templates run entirely inside Excel or Google Sheets on your device or account. Your project plans are never uploaded to us.

When you buy or download, our reseller collects what it needs to complete the order: your name, email address, country and payment details (we never see your card details). We receive your name, email address, country and order details so we can deliver the files, provide support and handle refunds.

Emails. We send order and product-update emails about what you downloaded. We send news and offers only if you opted in at checkout, and every email has an unsubscribe link.

We don't sell or share your personal data with anyone else. To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, email support@keelson.co.in.
