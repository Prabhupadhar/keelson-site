More questions.

Reports app

How many reports do I need for the slip trend?

Three for the milestone trend, two for the re-baseline log. If you have older exports, drop them in: each is placed by its status date, not the order you add them.

Can I change the wording before I send it?

Yes. Click any commentary to rewrite it, or download Word and edit there. The forecast's numbers can be hidden but not edited.

Is it PMBOK or PRINCE2 compliant?

It uses their vocabulary and the artefacts both expect: baselines, tolerances, registers and change control. No standards body certifies templates, and Keelson doesn't claim to be certified or endorsed.

Template packs

Can it really match my client's Excel template?

For templates with one row per task, yes: up to 20 columns in any order, fixed values, blank columns and five date styles. Merged cells or several rows per task still need a tidy-up after pasting.

Can I use it on client projects?

Yes, on any number of your own and your clients' projects. Share the filled-in files with anyone; don't resell or give away the templates themselves.

Do I need to enable macros?

No. Every workbook is a plain .xlsx with formulas only, so company security settings won't block it.

Will I get a native .mpp file?

No. You get MS Project XML, which MS Project desktop opens directly, plus an import tab. Microsoft Planner can't import either.
